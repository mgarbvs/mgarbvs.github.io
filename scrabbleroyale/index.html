Scrabble Royale

Words. Territory. Escalating damage.

How to play
  • Goal: be the last player standing.
  • Every word you play must connect to an existing tile — yours, an opponent's, or neutral.
  • Never played yet? Your word may instead cover your ★ spawn square, even in isolation.
  • Capturing a tile in your word deals damage split by how many of an opponent's tiles you captured.
  • It's real-time: tiles you lay down show to everyone as dashed pending tiles. Commit your word (Enter) after your cooldown — but if an opponent lands a word through your squares first, yours is blocked.
  • Damage escalates every 30 seconds; stay active or you'll slowly lose HP.
  • If a minute passes with no play landing anywhere, the game ends — highest HP wins.
